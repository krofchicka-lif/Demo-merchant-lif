Love It Forward makes it easy to act on that thought through a brand you love.

Commerce spent a generation making it easier to get. Love It Forward is building what comes next: an easier, more human way to give. Through the brands people already love, one person chooses another, tells them why and puts something toward whatever they choose. It begins anonymously, becomes personal at the reveal, leaves the choice with the receiver and brings gratitude back to the giver. When someone else comes to mind, a new act can begin. Love It Forward gives people a contemporary way to give good and feel good, and gives brands a trusted, measurable way to grow. This is a new behavior the right brands can help establish.
Have love. Will share.
